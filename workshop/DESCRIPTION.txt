[h1]Extra Ancestry Architecture[/h1]
[i]Breed the bloodline you actually want.[/i]

The [b]Architected Ancestry[/b] dynasty perk (Blood legacy, fourth perk) lets you pick one congenital trait to become more common in your dynasty. The base game gives you [b]8[/b] choices. This mod gives you [b]25[/b].

[h2]Your choices[/h2]
[b]Gifted blood[/b]
[list]
[*] Beautiful, Genius and Divine (all three at the top tier, III)
[*] Fecund, Pure-blooded
[*] Ambitious, Zealous
[/list]
[b]Strange blood[/b]
[list]
[*] Albino, Giant, Dwarf, Scaly, Spindly, Paranoid
[/list]
[b]Tainted blood[/b]
[list]
[*] Hideous, Imbecile, Feeble, Inbred
[*] Lunatic, Possessed, Depressed
[*] Wheezing, Bleeder, Club-footed, Hunchbacked, Stuttering
[/list]
Whether it is a dynasty of geniuses or a family curse, it is your choice.

[h2]Every choice now works[/h2]
The base game only hands out 8 of the perk's traits when a child is born. Any other trait you picked from a modded list was selectable but never appeared. This mod adds its own birth handler for all the traits it adds, using the base game's own rules:
[list]
[*] The child belongs to a dynasty that has the perk
[*] At least one real parent is of that dynasty
[*] A child who already has a conflicting trait is skipped
[*] Same odds as the base game: 5%, plus 5% when a parent is a player
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*] Made for Crusader Kings III [b]1.20 Crozier[/b]
[*] Replaces the base game's [b]blood_legacy_4[/b] perk, so it conflicts with other mods that change that perk, including [b]More Dynasty Perk Options[/b]. Use only one of them.
[*] Adds to the base game's birth hook instead of replacing it, so other birth mods keep working
[/list]
